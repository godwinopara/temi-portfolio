# Tèmítọ́pẹ́ Ọládòkun — blank-slate portfolio direction

## Purpose

A high-credibility personal platform for an AI product leader, speaker, and technologist. It should feel like a considered publication rather than a conventional “speaker site”: confident, intelligent, human, and quietly memorable. Primary action: email for speaking, press, and collaborations.

## Content architecture

Homepage: introduction → proof strip → selected work → speaking moments → writing → contact. Supporting routes: About, Work, Talks, Writing, Contact. Preserve truthful current content and the existing email address; do not invent awards, clients, testimonials, metrics, dates, or biographies.

## Visual system — Editorial Signal

- Tone: culturally aware contemporary editorial design; restrained but expressive, more art-book than SaaS dashboard.
- Canvas: warm off-white `#F4F0EA`; ink `#111111`; quiet gray `#77736D`; electric cobalt `#3156E8`; acid chartreuse `#DFFF4F`; vermilion `#FA5536`; hairline rules `#D8D2CA`.
- Type: modern neo-grotesk for all UI and headlines (use `Space Grotesk` or a close system fallback); a single italic serif accent only for 1–3 selected words at a time (Instrument Serif). No decorative display fonts, gradients, glassmorphism, or stock-tech imagery.
- Grid: 12-column editorial grid on desktop with abundant negative space; 4-column mobile. Maximum content width 1440px, outer gutters 24px mobile / 48px desktop.
- Shapes: precise circles, rectangular blocks, crop frames, lines, and oversized type; no blobs, floating ornaments, or generic “AI” motifs.
- Imagery: use the supplied portrait as the primary hero visual. Use real talk/work images as evidence cards, with intentional asymmetric cropping. Do not create fake photos or replace the portrait.
- UI: thin rules, sharp 2px radius or square corners, solid color fills, underlined text links, oversized circular arrow buttons. Avoid pill-heavy controls and excessive cards.

## Motion and access

- Motion should clarify hierarchy: a short 200–350ms entrance of section elements, hover underline/crop shifts, and a controlled horizontal ticker. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by disabling non-essential motion.
- Keep keyboard focus visible with a high-contrast 3px outline. Maintain semantic landmarks, coherent heading order, sufficient color contrast, descriptive alt text, and 44px minimum interactive targets.

## Homepage composition

1. Thin utility/nav bar: wordmark left, section links center/right, unmistakable “Email me” action.
2. Full-viewport manifesto hero: index label, a 3–5 word sharply written statement, portrait in a disciplined crop, and an animated-but-optional proof ticker.
3. Compact “works across” statement / capability matrix, using type and rule hierarchy rather than icon cards.
4. Selected work as two large editorial case-study slabs.
5. Speaking as a dark contrast band: two talk stills, large titles, deliberate CTA.
6. One featured article as a typography-first pull quote with cover image.
7. Big contact finale with the real email address.

## Non-negotiables

- Build a new information hierarchy and composition; do not mirror the prior component layout or present it as a recolor.
- Do not imitate Doug Melville’s exact layout, typography, or palette.
- No generic startup gradients, 3D mockups, dashboards, badge clouds, or fabricated social proof.
- Use only the colors, fonts, spacing, and component conventions specified above.
